Fig 0 · Annual Scientific Production

Annual publications + cumulative trajectory with decade distribution boxplots

tune Settings — Fig 0

Annual Scientific Production
Annual count (black) + cumulative publications (red) · Panel A
Panel A — Annual + Cumulative
Annual publications chart.
Panel B — Decade Distribution (Boxplot)
Annual publications (left axis)
Cumulative publications (right axis)
info Scientific Description
Objective: Visualize the temporal evolution of scientific production, identifying acceleration phases and growth patterns by decade.

Panel A: X-axis = year; Left Y-axis = annual count; Right Y-axis = cumulative total. Black line with markers = annual publications. Red line = cumulative growth curve, revealing exponential acceleration in recent decades.

Panel B: Boxplot distribution of annual publications per decade. Box = interquartile range (Q1–Q3); horizontal line = median; whiskers = min/max. Distinct colors per decade facilitate visual comparison of publication rate distributions.

Interpretation: Accelerating annual counts and cumulative curves approaching inflection points indicate a consolidating field. Increasing IQR range in recent decades signals growing research heterogeneity.

Raw articles (left) vs. fractionalized contribution (right)

Most Relevant Authors
Articles count (left) · Articles fractionalized (right)
Articles
Raw articles per author.
Articles Fractionalized
Fractionalized.
Color scale: N. Articles (low → high)

info Scientific Description
Objective: Identify the most prolific authors and assess their adjusted scholarly contribution via fractionalization.

Left panel — Articles: X-axis = number of publications; Y-axis = authors sorted descending. Bubble size and blue color gradient encode raw article count.

Right panel — Articles Fractionalized: Each article's contribution is divided by its total author count. Orange-to-dark-brown gradient. Authors with high fractionalized scores relative to raw count publish predominantly as first or sole authors.

Interpretation: Discrepancies between panels reveal collaboration patterns. High raw count / low fractionalized = large team co-authorship. The reverse identifies scholars with concentrated independent output.

Author timeline.

info Scientific Description
Objective: Map the temporal scholarly activity of the most productive authors, identifying career trajectories and citation efficiency over time.

Axes: X-axis = publication year; Y-axis = author name.

Visual encoding: Bubble size ∝ number of articles published that year. Color encodes citations per year (TC/yr): cool pinks = low citation rate; warm reds = high rate. A connecting line traces each author's timeline from first to last publication.

Interpretation: Authors with long timelines demonstrate sustained research commitment. Large, intensely colored bubbles mark high-impact publication events.
